Nick Anderson

I am an economics PhD candidate at The New School for Social Research. I work on public finance, banking and financial history, and the empirical methods that make long-run economic data usable — which mostly means going and getting the sources myself.

The rest of the ecosystem

The platform hosts Gerhard (US public finance, gerhard.heterodata.org), Shaikh Data (shaikh.heterodata.org — replications of Anwar Shaikh’s Capitalism and Measuring the Wealth of Nations), Lewis (international economics, lewis.heterodata.org), Leonid (Soviet statistics, leonid.heterodata.org), Leontief (US input–output, leontief.heterodata.org), StarCruiser (US employment and inflation, starcruiser.heterodata.org), and Erwin (military and defense economics — 322 series; the site is built but its public address is not routed yet, so there is nothing to link to). Alongside them are the affiliated standalone sites: freenic.org (banking data), volcker.org (my dissertation), foodberg.org (food prices), hmdaexplore.com (mortgage lending), methodex.fyi (statistical methodology), nycvisualizer.com (New York City transit and sidewalk accessibility), and jjmuni.com (municipal pedestrian infrastructure). Data downloads across the ecosystem are gathered at files.heterodata.org and indexed on the hub’s data page. The full list, with each project’s repository beside its site, is on the Projects page.
